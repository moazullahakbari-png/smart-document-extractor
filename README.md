# Less Confusing. More Focus.

Have files to sort through? Spend less time copying information by hand.

Smart Document Extractor lets you upload TXT, CSV, and text-based PDF files, review the results, and download them as CSV files you can open in Excel.

### What you can do
- Upload multiple supported files in one session.
- Extract field-and-value information from structured TXT files.
- View and process CSV data.
- Extract available text from text-based PDFs.
- Download the results as CSV files.

**Curious if it fits your workflow? Try it with a sample file.**

### Need more details?

**Current limits:** Scanned PDFs and images cannot be read yet. TXT extraction works best with simple entries such as `Name: Ahmad`. PDF text is extracted by page, and complex layouts may not retain their original structure. Please review results before using them for important work.

We’re working to improve the tool, with OCR and smarter extraction among the ideas for future versions.
